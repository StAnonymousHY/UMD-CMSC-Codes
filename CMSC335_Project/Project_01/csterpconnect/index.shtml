About Me

Hi, I’m Hanxuan (Hanson) Yang, an undergraduate double-majoring in Computer Science and Statistics at the University of Maryland, College Park. I’m passionate about building data-driven, practical solutions that combine software engineering and statistical insight, from full-stack applications (Vue + Spring Boot) to verification tools in Haskell and statistical modeling in Python and SAS. My internship at Danfoo Tech gave me hands-on experience developing manufacturing analytics dashboards, managing large datasets, and creating tools that improve decision-making on the factory floor.

But when VSCode is closed, I swap one set of challenges for another. I’m an excessive gamer — the type who can grind through Counter-Strike matches, drop into endless PUBG battle royales, and spend hours perfecting combos in Street Fighter 6. Gaming isn’t just a hobby for me; it’s a way of thinking. It sharpens my focus, trains me to adapt quickly, and pushes me to collaborate under pressure — skills I bring right back into my programming and project work.

I thrive in both worlds: solving bugs with the same persistence I bring to ranked matches, and approaching complex projects like strategic team plays. Whether it’s debugging, deploying, or defeating opponents, I’m always looking for the next challenge to level up.
